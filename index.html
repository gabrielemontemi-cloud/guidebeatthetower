Gameplay

  • Tap to drop blocks and stack them as precisely as possible.
  • Perfect drops build your combo and increase points.
  • Use shields to auto-rescue once and continue your run.

Power-ups

  • Double Score: temporarily doubles base points.
  • Fever: easier perfects, steady block width, boosted scoring.
  • Slow-Mo: slows the moving block for precision drops.
  • Jackpot: turns blocks gold and multiplies points significantly.
  • Maxi Block: next placement keeps full width (no trim) and counts as Perfect.
  • Rainbow Block: perfecting it grants a big bonus + effects.
  • Super Block: special block with extra Perfect bonus.
  • Shield: one-time auto-rescue on a miss.
